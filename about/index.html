Our Story

World-class performers, built for your event.

Dream World Cirque is a Los Angeles boutique aerial and cirque company. Our roster includes contortionists, aerialists, and dancers trained in silks, hoop, hammock, and trapeze — many with backgrounds in Cirque du Soleil and professional ballet companies. We don't book off-the-shelf packages; every act is designed from scratch around your venue, audience, and budget, whether that's a single aerial piece or a full multi-act production.

  • Custom-designed acts for weddings, corporate events, and broadcast work
  • In-house costume and rigging equipment rental
  • Trusted by NBC, USA Network, Disney, Mercedes, and Cisco
